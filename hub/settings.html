Name and logo

Leave this blank to use OpenPetChatz. The browser tab uses this name. The top of each page can show the name, a picture, or both.

PNG, JPEG, or GIF, up to 512 KB. A wide picture is scaled to fit. Without a picture, the name is shown. Put {logo} in the guest instructions where that picture should appear on the printout and in the email.

Guest instructions

{name}, {logo}, {camera}, {expires}, {address}, {username}, and {password} are filled in when the guest account is created. {logo} is the picture, on the printout and in the email. {address} is the public web address, or a blank line when that name is not set. The instructions need {username} and {password}.

People away from home open https:// and this name. A proxy on your network can handle that page. The camera keeps using the address inside the house.

Call relay

A call inside the house still prefers a direct path. Use one cloud relay plus this hub. The camera gathers addresses on one core, so turning both cloud relays on at once makes that slower.

Uses the TURN ports forwarded to this machine.

Mail

Used to email a guest sign-in, and to email activity. The password is stored on this hub and is not shown again after you save it.

Errors are sign-in failures, refused calls, and setup that did not finish. Passwords are not included. A guest sign-in email is separate, and that message does include the new password.

Tasks

Each task runs once a day at the time you choose, using the house time zone from Schedule. Turning one on after today's time has passed waits until tomorrow. Run now does it immediately.
