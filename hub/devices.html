Camera screen

No picture. The screen shows Out of service.

JPEG. 320 by 240 pixels matches the camera panel. The camera's browser window is wider than that panel, so the picture is drawn at 320 by 240. A larger picture is scaled and cropped to fill it. Keep the file under 1 MB. The same two pictures are used on every camera. With no picture saved, the screen shows Ready or Out of service.

Cameras

The name is what people see. The line under each camera is its id and the last address it used to reach this hub. Picture size is Custom, 480p, or 720p. Custom is the default and keeps the width and height boxes. 480p is 640 by 480. 720p is 1280 by 720. A saved 480p or 720p stays selected. Any other size stays on Custom. The size and frame rate are used the next time that camera starts a call. 320 by 240 at 12 is the safe starting point on these boards. Removing a camera clears its name, settings, and who can call it. A camera that is still powered on comes back with those defaults. Guide

Set up a camera

Scan looks at the hub's home network, up to 254 addresses. It lists a PetChatz that still accepts the empty root password it shipped with, and marks one that already points at this hub. Set up installs the watchdog change, points the camera at this hub, and reloads its screen. A camera that is already set up is left running.
