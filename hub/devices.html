Set up a camera

One press installs the watchdog change, points the camera at this hub, and reloads its screen. The camera must be on the home network and still use the empty root password it shipped with. A camera that is already set up is left running.

JPEG. 320 by 240 pixels matches the camera panel. The camera's browser window is wider than that panel, so the picture is drawn at 320 by 240. A larger picture is scaled and cropped to fill it. Keep the file under 1 MB. The same two pictures are used on every camera. With no picture saved, the screen shows Ready or Out of service.

Cameras

The name is what people see. The line under each camera is its id and the last address it used to reach this hub. Picture size and frame rate are used the next time that camera starts a call. 320 by 240 at 12 is the safe starting point on these boards. Removing a camera clears its name, settings, and who can call it. A camera that is still powered on comes back with those defaults.
