How to place a call.

The caller steps are above. The rest of this page is for an admin.

For people who call

Sign in with the username and password you were given. You cannot change that password yourself. Ask an admin if you need a new one.

After you sign in, Calls is the only page.

Place a call

  1. Open the web address you were given and sign in.
  2. Find the camera. It says Ready for call, Device Offline, or Device outside active hours. A picture can stand in for the ready and closed words when an admin has saved one. Offline stays as words.
  3. Press Call. On a phone the call fills the screen. On a computer, stay on the page. The sound has to start from that press.
  4. The line under the picture shows how long the call has lasted.
  5. Mute turns off your microphone. Camera off turns off your camera. The pet camera does not flip or turn off.
  6. Camera volume changes how loud the pet camera is for you.
  7. Treat and Scent appear only when an admin has turned them on for that camera. Treat sends one treat. Scent runs the fan for about 10 seconds. Either button can be missing, or stay off, outside the camera's hours or after the daily limit.
  8. Press Hang up. Closing the tab also ends the call.

One person can be in a call with a camera at a time. If your account is turned off, or its date has passed, the call ends and the sign-in stops working. A guest sign-in works through the end of the date on the printed page.

For administrators

You see every tab and you can call every camera. A normal account can call only the cameras you check, and only sees Calls.

People

Create an account, check its cameras, turn it on or off, and set when access ends. An empty end time means it does not expire. Turning it off, deleting it, or a date that has passed signs that person out and ends a call they were in.

Delete account removes that person. The account you are signed in with cannot be deleted, and at least one admin who can still sign in has to remain.

A user cannot change a password. Set password on that person's card changes it and signs them out. My password, at the bottom, changes the admin account you are using, after you type the current password. Random password fills in 8 characters and shows them so you can copy them.

Guest account asks for one camera, the date access ends, and an email if you want one. The hub makes a username and password and opens a page you can print. If mail is saved on Settings, the same page is emailed. The cleanup task removes only these guest accounts, after that date. Accounts from New account stay until you delete them.

Devices

Name each camera and set its picture size, frame rate, echo cancellation, and noise suppression. The line under a camera is its id and the last address it used to reach this hub.

Deploy to this camera uses the camera's address on the home network. It installs the watchdog change, points the camera at this hub, and reloads its screen. The camera must still use the empty root password it shipped with. A camera that is already set up is left running.

Treat and Scent start off. Turn one on and save, and Calls shows that button. One scent press runs the fan for about 10 seconds.

Camera screen sets two pictures used by every camera: one while it is ready, and one while it is outside its hours. The panel is 320 by 240. A larger picture is scaled and cropped. With no picture, the screen keeps the Ready or Out of service words.

Remove clears the saved name, settings, and who can call that camera. A camera that is still on comes back with the defaults.

Schedule

Set the house time zone, when each camera can be used, and how many treats and scent releases are allowed in 24 hours. With no windows, a camera can be used any time. A "can be used" window limits it to those times. A "cannot be used" window blocks it, including inside an open window. Windows can repeat every week, or apply to one date. A window that ends earlier than it starts continues through midnight.

Callers are told when the next open time starts. The camera screen says Out of service during a closed window and Ready when it opens. A picture from Devices replaces that text. A call already underway is left in place. Treat and scent stay off outside an open window. Each limit starts at 12.

Activity

Sign-ins, calls, treats, scent, and changes. Filter by a username, a camera, or both. Each entry includes the address the person connected from. Passwords are never written here. The newest 10,000 entries are kept.

Settings

Name is the title on the pages, on the guest printout, and in email. Leave it blank to keep OpenPetChatz.

Guest instructions are the printed page and the email. {name}, {camera}, {expires}, {address}, {username}, and {password} are filled in for each guest. The text has to keep {username} and {password}. Restore the starting text puts the original wording back. Save to keep it.

How people open the page is a web address you publish, or a Cloudflare Tunnel. The tunnel carries the page only. Calls still need a relay. Leave this hub's relay on for calls inside the house, and add one cloud relay when callers are away. After a key or token is saved, the page shows only its last four characters.

Mail sends the guest sign-in and, if you turn it on, activity. Activity mail can be off, errors only, or every activity. Those messages do not include passwords. The guest email does. Send a test checks the server.

Tasks run once a day at the time you choose, in the house time zone. Delete expired guest accounts is off until you turn it on. Run now does it immediately. Other accounts are not removed by that task.
