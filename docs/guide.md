# User's guide

This is the short guide for someone who calls a camera. On the hub, Help shows only this page unless an admin is signed in. An admin also sees [admin-guide.md](admin-guide.md) on that same Help page. The heading starts as OpenPetChatz until an admin changes it. It can be that name, a picture, or both.

## For people who call

You sign in with the username and password you were given. After that, the only page you see is Calls. You do not see the other tabs.

You cannot change your own password. Ask an admin if you need a new one.

## Place a call

1. Open the web address you were given and sign in.
2. Find the camera. It says Ready for call, Device Offline, or Device outside active hours. A picture can stand in for Ready for call or Device outside active hours when an admin has saved one. Offline stays as words.
3. Press Call. On a phone the call fills the screen. On a computer, stay on the page. The browser has to start the sound from that press, so use the Call button rather than a control that appears later.
4. The line under the picture shows how long the call has lasted.
5. Mute turns off your microphone. Camera off turns off your camera. The pet camera does not flip or turn off.
6. Camera volume changes how loud the pet camera is on your side.
7. Treat and Scent appear only when an admin has turned them on for that camera. Treat sends one treat. Scent runs the fan for about 10 seconds. Either button can be missing, or stay off, when the camera is outside its hours or has already been used as many times as allowed that day.
8. Press Hang up. Closing the tab also ends the call.

One person can be in a call with a camera at a time. If someone else is already connected, wait until that call ends.

If your account is turned off, or the date on it has passed, the call ends and the sign-in stops working. A guest sign-in works through the end of the date on the printed page, then stops.
